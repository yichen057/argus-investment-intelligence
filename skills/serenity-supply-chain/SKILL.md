# Procedure

1. Separate structural demand from a normal cycle.
2. Describe the architecture or technology transition.
3. Map the full supply chain before selecting companies.
4. Identify bottlenecks and credible substitutes.
5. Map companies using primary evidence.
6. Evaluate revenue and valuation elasticity.
7. Retrieve supporting and opposing evidence.
8. Define observable invalidation conditions.
9. Classify candidates as Recommend, Watch, Avoid, or Insufficient Evidence.

# Evidence Rules

- Core facts require filings, regulators, customers, or other primary sources.
- Broker research and news may generate leads but cannot alone justify high
  conviction.
- Every material claim requires evidence IDs.
- Historical work must honor `as_of_date`.

# Output

- thesis;
- supply-chain map;
- bottleneck ranking;
- company mapping;
- evidence table;
- bull/base/bear cases;
- counter-evidence;
- falsification list;
- monitoring plan.
